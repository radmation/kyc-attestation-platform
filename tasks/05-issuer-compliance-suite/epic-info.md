## Epic 05: Issuer-Centric Compliance Suite

**Objective**: Extend the platform's capabilities beyond end-user KYC to provide a full suite of compliance tools for token issuers, addressing their direct regulatory obligations for treasury management, reserve verification, and regulatory reporting.

**Business Goal**: Transition the platform from a specialized KYC/AML solution into a comprehensive, indispensable Compliance-as-a-Service (CaaS) provider for the digital asset industry. Solidify the "Identhor" brand as the market leader in regulatory technology for token issuers.

---

### Key Features & Modules

1.  **Treasury & Reserve Attestation:**
    *   Provide automated, on-chain "Proof-of-Reserves" by integrating with both on-chain sources (treasury wallets) and off-chain sources (bank accounts via custodians or data aggregators).
    *   Generate periodic, immutable attestations of an issuer's solvency and backing.

2.  **Advanced Regulatory Reporting Dashboard:**
    *   Create a secure portal for issuers to generate, review, and submit regulatory filings.
    *   Offer pre-built templates for reports required under frameworks like the GENIUS Act.
    *   Provide a secure, read-only "auditor view" for regulators.

---

### Phases

*   **Phase 1: Reserve Attestation Foundation (P1-RES)**
    *   Focus on the core mechanics of verifying and attesting to reserves. This includes research into financial data APIs and building the on-chain attestation smart contracts for reserves.

*   **Phase 2: Reporting & Dashboard (P2-REP)**
    *   Build the issuer-facing dashboard and the reporting engine that consumes data from the Reserve Attestation module and other platform services.

---

### Success Metrics

*   **Issuer Adoption**: 50% of existing clients adopt the Reserve Attestation module within 6 months of launch.
*   **Time Savings**: Reduce the time spent on manual reserve reporting for clients by 90%.
*   **Audit Efficiency**: Positive feedback from a pilot group of auditors on the clarity and utility of the secure audit portal.
*   **Revenue Growth**: Introduce a new pricing tier for the CaaS suite, leading to a 25% increase in Average Revenue Per User (ARPU).
